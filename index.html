CASE STUDY 02

STROBEL DENTISTRY

SEO Copywriting & Premium Positioning
Brief: reposition a century-old Chicago practice as an elevated, modern experience, without losing the heritage that makes it credible.
Visit strobeldentistry.com →
THE HOMEPAGE, AS WRITTEN
“Elevated care, backed by science.”
Trusted Downtown Chicago Dentists. Family-owned for three generations.
strobeldentistry.com
Strobel has practiced in the same Loop building since 1932. That's real, ownable authority most dental sites have to manufacture. The challenge was pairing that heritage with a premium, modern positioning without drifting into "stuffy legacy practice" on one side or "generic dental site" on the other. "Elevated care backed by science" carries both: the confidence of a hotel brand and the credibility of a specialty practice.
strobeldentistry.com
“Our Chicago dentists pair modern technology and advanced, specialized training with concierge-level service.”

Strategy

The heritage line ("Since 1932") does the trust-building most dental sites spend paragraphs on. That frees the rest of the copy to sell the modern experience: technology, comfort, concierge service, without re-litigating credibility on every page.

Impact

A positioning built to compete with Chicago's other downtown dental brands on tone, not only on services. Premium without being cold. Established without feeling dated.

CASE STUDY 03

AUDUBON DENTAL CARE

UX & Conversion Strategy
This case study is about structure more than headlines: what a visitor sees first, and why.
Visit audubondentalcenterofclinton.com →

Brief

Increase new-patient appointments by restructuring the site around how patients actually decide, not only by rewriting the words already on the page.

My Role

Partnered with the client and project team to identify the real decision drivers, then rebuilt the information architecture: what visitors see first, what earns its own page, and how many paths lead to booking.

The Fix

Emergency care, insurance acceptance, and new-patient pricing existed somewhere on the old site, just not where or when patients needed them. Each got a dedicated page built around one specific search, and the strongest offer moved into the homepage's first screen.

How the structure changed

The rewrite wasn't only softer language. It meant fewer steps between a worried parent searching "emergency dentist near me" and a booked appointment. Each landing page answers one search intent completely, so a visitor never has to dig through a general services page to find out if they're covered or what a visit costs. That's an information-architecture decision as much as a writing one.

audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
Three dedicated pages came out of this restructure: emergency care, new-patient pricing, and insurance. Each one is built to answer a single high-intent search completely, with its own headline, offer, and path to booking.
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
“New here? Get started for $149. No insurance? No problem.”
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
“Same-day appointments available. Emergency care when you need it.”
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
“Fast relief starts with a $99 emergency visit.”

Why It Works

Each page's headline names the exact objection stopping someone from booking, whether that's no insurance, dental anxiety, or cost, and answers it in the same breath, on a page built for that one visitor.

Impact

The client reported a measurable increase in new-patient appointments and said the site finally reflected how patients actually decide.

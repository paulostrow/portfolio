STROBEL DENTISTRY

SEO Copywriting & Premium Positioning
Brief: Reposition a century-old Chicago practice as an elevated, modern experience, without losing the heritage that makes it credible.
Visit strobeldentistry.com →
THE HOMEPAGE, AS WRITTEN
“Elevated care, backed by science.”
Trusted Downtown Chicago Dentists. Family-owned for three generations.
strobeldentistry.com
Strobel has practiced in the same Loop building since 1932. That's real, ownable authority most dental sites have to manufacture.
strobeldentistry.com
strobeldentistry.com
“After nearly a century in The Loop, we're still moving forward.”

Strategy

The heritage line ("Since 1932") does the trust-building most dental sites spend paragraphs on. That frees the rest of the copy to sell the modern experience: technology, comfort, concierge service, without re-litigating credibility on every page.

Impact

A positioning built to compete with Chicago's other downtown dental brands on tone, not only on services. Premium without being cold. Established without feeling dated.

CASE STUDY 01

SMILELOUNGE

SEO Copywriting & Brand Positioning
Brief: reposition a Brooklyn practice to attract a younger audience and stand out in a saturated market — without losing clinical trust.
THE HOMEPAGE, AS WRITTEN
“Your Neighborhood Greenpoint Dentists.”
Care you'll keep coming back to.
smileloungebk.com
The client asked for "the best website the agency had ever built" in one of the most competitive dental markets in the country. Every headline pairs neighborhood + service — "Greenpoint Dentists," "Greenpoint-owned and operated" — a local-SEO pattern that targets exactly how people search, while the voice stays playful enough to sound like the brand, not a keyword list.
smileloungebk.com
“Where great taste meets great teeth.”
smileloungebk.com
“Treat Yourself. Our Studio Specials.”
smileloungebk.com
“A secret too good to keep.”

SEO & Conversion Strategy

Every headline does double duty — searchable service and neighborhood terms up top, brand personality right underneath. "Treat Yourself" sells a discount without reading like a keyword.

Impact

The client called it the best site the agency had built, and became a vocal advocate — referring new business off the strength of this project alone.

CASE STUDY 02

AUDUBON DENTAL CARE

Local SEO & Conversion Optimization
Brief: grow new-patient appointments by surfacing the factors that actually drive the choice of dentist — emergency care, insurance, and price.
THE HOMEPAGE, AS WRITTEN
“Exceptional care. Modern technology. Smiles you can trust.”
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
The messaging that actually gets someone to book — same-day emergency care, insurance fit, first-visit price — wasn't consistently surfaced across the site. I built and wrote dedicated landing pages around the exact high-intent searches patients run ("Clinton emergency dentist," "$149 new patient special"), then rewrote homepage copy to reinforce the same terms in plain, reassuring language.
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
“New here? Get started for $149. No insurance? No problem.”
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
“Same-day appointments available. Emergency care when you need it.”
audubondentalcenterofclinton.com
“Fast relief starts with a $99 emergency visit.”

SEO & Conversion Strategy

Each page is built around one high-intent search — emergency dentist, new patient special, insurance accepted — and each headline answers the objection behind that search in the same breath.

Impact

The client reported a measurable increase in new-patient appointments and said the site finally reflected how patients actually decide.

CASE STUDY 03

COLUMBIA DENTAL GROUP

SEO Copywriting & Service-Page Differentiation
Brief: reposition the practice as an Invisalign specialist by making its airway-focused approach the site's central story.
THE HOMEPAGE, AS WRITTEN
“Your partner in lasting dental health.”
columbiadentalgroup.com
Invisalign is offered everywhere, so "we offer Invisalign" isn't a story — or a way to rank against every other practice targeting the same search. "Columbia's go-to Invisalign dentist" and a dedicated Invisalign page structure go after that exact query directly, while the airway-health angle gives the copy a reason to be clicked over a competitor's.
columbiadentalgroup.com/invisalign
“Columbia's go-to Invisalign dentist. Proudly an Invisalign Gold+ Provider.”
columbiadentalgroup.com/invisalign
“The clear path to a perfect smile.”
columbiadentalgroup.com
“State-of-the-art care with a personal touch.”

SEO & Conversion Strategy

Every Invisalign headline targets the treatment-plus-location search directly, then ties the treatment back to health, not just appearance — a reason to click this practice over any other Invisalign provider in the results.

Impact

Invisalign became the clear centerpiece of the site, matched to the client's strategic goals — and a client enthusiastic about the direction.
